### 🚀 About Me

I'm a **cloud and platform architect** and co-founder of [SIPATECH](https://sipatech.io) - with 20+ years of experience building **cloud-native, multi-cloud, and AI-ready platforms** across enterprises and startups.

My work brings together **DevOps, Platform Engineering, and AI Infrastructure**, helping organizations design **scalable, resilient, secure, and cost-optimized systems** that accelerate innovation.

Recently, I've been deeply focused on architecting **GPU-powered, multi-cloud AI infrastructure** - across two US-based AI startups:

- **Confidential AI Startup (Stealth Mode)** - Designed and built a production-grade, multi-cloud AI infrastructure platform across **GCP and Azure** - GPU-enabled Kubernetes clusters, unified IaC using **Terraform** and **Helm**, distributed AI pipelines using **Ray**, **Istio**, and **Temporal**, and a full cross-cloud observability stack. This engagement sparked the conviction to co-found SIPATECH.

- **Confidential AI Startup** - Designed and deployed an **Amazon Bedrock-based AI infrastructure** on AWS - spanning multiple **EKS clusters** with a centralised observability platform built on the **LGTM stack** ([Loki](https://grafana.com/oss/loki/), [Grafana](https://grafana.com/), [Tempo](https://grafana.com/oss/tempo/), [Mimir](https://grafana.com/oss/mimir/)) running in Kubernetes.

I specialize in **Kubernetes (EKS, GKE, AKS)**, **Terraform**, **Helm**, **GitOps**, and **Grafana Stack–based observability**, while leveraging **automation, autoscaling**, and **FinOps strategies** to balance performance, security, and cost efficiency.
